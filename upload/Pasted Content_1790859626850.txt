3. Cellular Stage
3.1 Overview
The Cellular Stage represents the beginning of your evolutionary journey, where players control a
single microscopic cell competing for survival in primordial waters. This stage focuses on resource
collection (glucose, protein, lipid, elements), organelle acquisition, and cell division. The choices made
here fundamentally shape how your cell operates and what evolutionary paths become available in
later stages. Players must balance energy production, movement capabilities, defense mechanisms,
and reproductive capacity to thrive in a hostile microscopic world.
3.2 Organelle Categories (40+ Organelles)
3.2.1 Core Eukaryotic Organelles
The Core Eukaryotic Organelles represent the fundamental machinery of advanced cellular life. The
Nucleus serves as the command center, storing DNA and enabling evolution itself, making it one of the
most critical organelles for progression. The Nucleolus enhances ribosome production by 15%,
accelerating protein synthesis. The Rough Endoplasmic Reticulum and Smooth Endoplasmic Reticulum
specialize in protein and lipid synthesis respectively, each providing a 10% boost to their respective
outputs. The Golgi Apparatus packages proteins for transport, improving overall cellular efficiency by
10%. Mitochondria generate ATP for energy, providing a 10% speed boost, while Peroxisomes handle
detoxification and offer 5% toxin resistance. The Lysosome grants +3 attack through digestive
enzymes, and the Vacuole provides 50% additional storage capacity. Ribosomes enhance protein
synthesis by 5%, and the Cytoskeleton variants improve movement precision and cell division
capabilities.
3.2.2 Movement Organelles
Movement Organelles determine how effectively your cell can navigate its environment and escape
predators. The Flagellum provides a significant +20 speed boost through whip-like propulsion. Cilia
offer +15 speed along with the ability to manipulate fluid currents, useful for gathering resources. The
Pseudopod grants +10 speed and enables engulfment of smaller cells, opening up predatory gameplay

maneuverability, and feeding capabilities.
3.2.3 Attack/Defense Organelles
Attack and Defense Organelles define your cell's combat capabilities and survival strategies. The Pilus
provides +5 attack with puncture capability, ideal for aggressive cells. The Toxin Vacuole deals damage
over time to enemies, creating a debuff-focused combat style. The Cell Wall offers +2 defense as a
passive protective barrier. Bioluminescence provides a unique utility combination, attracting
resources while confusing predators with light displays. These options allow players to specialize their
cells as aggressive hunters, defensive survivors, or resource-gathering specialists.
3.2.4 Energy/Specialty Organelles
Energy and Specialty Organelles provide alternative metabolic strategies. The Chloroplast generates
passive glucose during daytime, enabling photosynthetic gameplay. The Thermoplast provides health
regeneration and heat energy utilization for cells in thermal environments. The Chemoplast allows
energy extraction from chemicals, opening up extreme environment colonization. These organelles
fundamentally change how players approach resource gathering, shifting from active hunting to
passive collection or environmental exploitation.
3.2.5 Plant-like, Animal-like, Fungal, and Protist-like Organelles
Specialized organelle paths allow cells to evolve toward specific archetypes. Plant-like organelles
include the Central Vacuole for massive storage and turgor pressure, Chromoplast for pigment
storage, and Amyloplast for starch storage. Animal-like organelles feature Centrioles and Centrosome
for faster cell division, and Acrosome for enhanced attack capabilities. Fungal organelles include
Woronin Bodies for damage sealing and Spitzenkorper for directed growth. Protist-like organelles
offer Contractile Vacuole for osmoregulation, Eyespot for light sensing, Trichocysts for defense
discharge, and Hydrogenosome for anaerobic ATP generation.
3.3 Archetype Specializations
Each of the seven archetypes begins with unique cellular advantages that influence gameplay from the
earliest moments. Terrestrial cells have balanced stats suitable for land-based evolution. Aquatic cells
excel in water environments with enhanced swimming capabilities. Amphibious cells possess
adaptations for both environments. Fungal cells gain bonuses to decomposition and research.
Arboreal cells benefit from photosynthesis-related bonuses. Subterranean cells have enhanced
burrowing and sensing capabilities. Aerial cells, while unusual at this stage, possess lightweight
structures that will later enable flight.

4. Creature Stage
4.1 Overview
The Creature Stage represents the transition from single-celled organism to multicellular complexity.
Players control an evolving creature navigating a 2D world, hunting for food, avoiding predators,
finding mates, and gradually developing the adaptations that will define their species. This stage
features 27 technologies across 3 development paths, with 6 unique archetypal technologies that
shape the evolutionary trajectory of your species. The core mechanics emphasize adaptation
selection, hunting and gathering, nest building, and reproduction mechanics.
4.2 Development Paths
4.2.1 Movement Adaptations Path
The Movement Adaptations path focuses on locomotion and environmental traversal. This path
begins with Legs providing +15 speed for terrestrial movement, the most common starting point for
land-based creatures. Wings unlock aerial capabilities with +25 speed and glide ability, opening
entirely new strategic options for resource gathering and escape. Fins provide +20 water speed for
aquatic-focused evolution. Tail offers +5 speed with improved balance, useful for climbing and
jumping. Webbed Feet enable amphibious gameplay with +15 swimming speed. Hooves provide +10
land speed with the ability to traverse rough terrain that would slow other creatures. This path defines
how your creature interacts with the physical world and what environments become accessible.
4.2.2 Combat Adaptations Path
The Combat Adaptations path determines your creature's effectiveness in hunting and self-defense.
Claws provide +8 attack as versatile melee weapons suitable for both hunting and combat. Teeth offer
+5 attack with consistent damage output. Horns grant +6 attack with charge attack capability, enabling
hit-and-run tactics. Venom Glands introduce poison damage over time, creating a debuff-focused
combat style that weakens prey or predators. Spines provide thorns damage to attackers, turning
defense into offense. Beaks offer +4 attack with precision strikes, useful for targeting specific weak
points. This path creates meaningful choices between burst damage, sustained damage, and defensive
capabilities.
4.2.3 Survival Adaptations Path
The Survival Adaptations path encompasses defense, senses, and special abilities that enhance
survivability. Armor provides +10 defense but may slow movement. Camouflage offers 15% stealth for
ambush hunting and predator avoidance. Shell grants +15 defense at the cost of -5 speed, ideal for
slow, defensive creatures. Spikes provide 20% damage reflection, punishing attackers. Mimicry allows
your creature to appear as other species, enabling deception-based gameplay. Sense adaptations
include enhanced Vision (+30%), Echolocation for detecting hidden creatures, Thermal Sense for
detecting warm bodies, and Electroreception for sensing life in water. Special abilities include
